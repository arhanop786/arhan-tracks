Privacy Policy — Arhan Tracks

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Arhan Tracks is a clinic queue tracker run by Arhan Family Clinic ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data we handle

  • Patients: name, mobile number, optional year of birth, and an optional note — used only to identify your place in the clinic queue and send you queue updates.
  • Queue activity: check-in times, token numbers, consultation start/end times, and aggregated wait statistics.
  • Staff: name, mobile number, and role — used for signing in to staff consoles.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location, or contacts.

Where data lives

Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL, with encryption in transit) and protected by row-level security. Your device also keeps a local offline copy so the app works without a connection.

Public queue display

The public live-queue view shows token numbers and statuses only — never patient names or phone numbers.

Retention & deletion

Queue data is cleared by the clinic as needed. To have your personal data deleted, contact the clinic at the number shown in the app.

Contact

Arhan Family Clinic — contact details are shown on the app's Profile screen, or reach us at the clinic front desk.
